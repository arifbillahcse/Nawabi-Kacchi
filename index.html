Our Story

Born in Bangladesh,
Beloved in the UK

Nawabi Kacchi was founded with a simple mission — to bring the soul of Bangladeshi cooking to Britain. Our chefs are masters of their craft, trained in the kitchens of Dhaka's most celebrated establishments.

Every dish we serve is a tribute to our roots. We use traditional clay pots, hand-ground spices, and slow-cooking methods that simply cannot be rushed.
