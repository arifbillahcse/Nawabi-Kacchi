Who We Are

More Than a Restaurant —
A Living Tradition

Nawabi Kacchi was founded in 2012 by the Hossain family, who moved from Dhaka to London carrying nothing but their heritage and an unwavering belief: that the finest Bangladeshi food deserved a world-class stage.

Our name says everything. "Nawabi" — meaning royal, noble, of the highest order. "Kacchi" — the legendary slow-cooked biryani technique that defines Bangladeshi culinary mastery. Together, they represent our promise: every dish we serve is fit for royalty.

Over twelve years, we have grown from a small family kitchen to one of London's most celebrated South Asian restaurants — without ever compromising on the recipes, the ingredients, or the love that goes into every plate.

Our Journey

Milestones That Made Us

2012

The Beginning

The Hossain family opens a small 30-seat restaurant on Brick Lane, London. The Kacchi Biryani sells out on the very first day — and every day since.

2014

First Award

Nawabi Kacchi wins "Best New Restaurant" at the London Asian Food Festival. The queue stretches around the block every weekend.

2016

Expansion

We double in size, expanding to a 80-seat dining hall with a private event space — perfect for weddings, corporate dinners, and family celebrations.

2019

Chef's Table Launch

We introduce the exclusive Chef's Table experience — a 6-course tasting menu personally curated by Head Chef Karim, showcasing the full spectrum of Bangladeshi cuisine.

2022

10th Anniversary

We celebrate a decade of Nawabi Kacchi with a sold-out Grand Nawabi Feast, donating all profits to the Bangladesh Flood Relief Fund.

2025

Best in London

Nawabi Kacchi is awarded "Best Bangladeshi Restaurant in London 2025" at the UK South Asian Food Awards. The journey continues.
