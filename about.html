Our Journey

Born in Dhaka,
Beloved in Britain

In 2012, our founder Anwar Hossain arrived in London with two suitcases, a century-old family recipe for Kacchi Biryani, and an impossible dream — to serve his grandmother's cooking to the world.

A decade later, Nawabi Kacchi has become the UK's most celebrated Bangladeshi restaurant — a haven where every spice tells a story, and every meal feels like Sunday lunch at nani's house.

We don't just sell food. We serve memory.

Our Milestones

A Decade in the Making

2012

A Single Kitchen on Brick Lane

We opened our doors with 8 tables, one clay oven, and a dream — to bring authentic Bangladeshi food to Britain.

2015

First 'Best Bangladeshi Restaurant' Award

Recognised by the British Curry Awards — a proud moment that proved our heritage has a home in the UK.

2018

Expansion & Tandoor Kitchen

Doubled our seating and installed a traditional coal tandoor — for breads and kebabs as they were meant to be.

2021

Featured in BBC Food

Our Kacchi Biryani named one of "Britain's 50 Must-Try Dishes" by BBC Food. Chef Rahim blushed for a week.

2023

Second Location Opens in Birmingham

We opened our doors in Birmingham's Balti Triangle — the same recipes, the same love, a whole new city to feed.

2026

Our Nawabi Family Keeps Growing

Over 50,000 guests served, a loyal community, and many more decades of royal feasts ahead. Thank you for dining with us.
