The Legend of Kacchi Biryani: How a Royal Dish Conquered the World

Long before Nawabi Kacchi opened its doors in London, Kacchi Biryani was already a legend. Born in the grand kitchens of Mughal-era Dhaka, this dish was once reserved exclusively for royalty — its name "Kacchi" literally meaning "raw", referring to the unique technique of layering uncooked marinated meat directly beneath fragrant basmati rice before sealing the pot with dough and slow-cooking over a gentle flame for hours.

Today, we bring that same tradition to every table at Nawabi Kacchi. Our chefs refuse to take shortcuts. Every pot is sealed. Every batch is slow-cooked. Every plate is a piece of living history.
