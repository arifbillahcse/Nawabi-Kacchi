The Secret Behind Authentic Kacchi Biryani — A 200-Year-Old Recipe

Ever wondered why our Kacchi Biryani tastes like nothing else in the UK? It's not magic — it's mastery. Our Head Chef, Ustad Rahim, shares the centuries-old Nawabi technique of layering raw marinated mutton with fragrant basmati, sealed with dough and slow-cooked over coal for four hours. One bite, and you're dining in 19th-century Dhaka.
